# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, establishment owners, and residential property owners in Negros Occidental who need a dependable local partner to install, maintain, or repair CCTV systems.

## Product Purpose

The website widens discovery for BI-Vision Networks and turns visitors into qualified enquiries through free-assessment, phone, email, and Facebook contact paths. It explains services clearly, demonstrates real installation capability, and builds confidence without inventing credentials or customer claims.

## Brand Personality

Protective, dependable, and local. The voice balances approachable hands-on service with disciplined technical expertise. The emotional goal is calm confidence: visitors should feel that their property will be assessed carefully and their system will be installed correctly.

## Anti-references

Avoid hacker or cyberpunk surveillance aesthetics, generic SaaS landing-page templates, luxury black-and-gold styling, excessive glow, glassmorphism, fake metrics, unverified awards, fabricated testimonials, and technical language that obscures the service.

## Design Principles

- Show verified work rather than making broad claims.
- Make contacting a technician obvious at every stage.
- Explain technical capability in plain, useful language.
- Balance local approachability with installation discipline.
- Design mobile-first for customers contacting the business from their phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px minimum touch targets, semantic landmarks and headings, useful alternative text, readable 16px-or-larger body copy, and reduced-motion support.
